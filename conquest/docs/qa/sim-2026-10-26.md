## Status (fix/conquest, 2026-10-27)
Findings: borders *did* change early (224 provinces by y46 on seed 1) but froze after ~y20 because the AI almost never declared war: manpower < 40% (armies ate it), allies counted 60% in the 1.4x strength ratio, 2-year cooldown. Peace deals themselves transfer provinces fine.
Fixes (ai.js, diplomacy.js, invariants.js): war gates relaxed (ally weight 0.3, ratio 1.25, cooldown 1.5y, manpower floor 25%); standing army capped at 4% (peace) / 15% (war) of max manpower in regiments, weakest army disbanded at peace when >1.5x cap; rumps (<=3 provinces) can be annexed outright; `makeVassal` now removes the alliance on both sides (was the source of all non-mutual alliances); QA invariant exempts the Mongol horde (it spawns without provinces by design).

### Status update: tick time (fix/conquest)
Profiling (20y, seed 2): ~75% of late-game tick time was `findPath` from `orderMove`, called by `aiArmy`/`manageFront` every day/3 days for targets with no route. Fix: `aiArmy` waits 10 days after a failed order; `orderMove` remembers an unreachable target (same army location) for 15 days (`a.noRoute`, JSON-safe).
Result (20y, seeds 1,2; ms/day at y20): 22.9/21.9 -> 13.7/17.8. Province turnover measured over 20y on seed 2: 131 peaces, 93 with provinces, 360 provinces changed hands (CUM +27, PLT -20, ENG +19, GAL -18 ...) i.e. gradual change; the largest realm (ALM) is stable at ~860 because its neighbours are weak/allied, not because wars don't transfer land. Remaining: 1 transient 0-regiment army per ~20y run, still above the 10 ms budget.
