# Progression (`ws/progression`)
Owns new files `js/sim/progression.js` (ProgressionMixin) and `js/data/innovations.js`, plus a self-contained progression panel appended to the UI.

1. Innovations (the medieval "tech tree"): ~60 innovations in eras (Early High Medieval 1200 → High Medieval 1250 → Late Medieval 1300+), in branches military (crossbow, plate, trebuchet, longbow), civic (universities, Roman law, double-entry bookkeeping), agriculture (heavy plough, three-field rotation, windmill), maritime (cog, compass). Research is driven by learning (court chaplain), development, universities and cultural spread from neighbours. Innovations unlock units, buildings, laws and modifiers through the existing `n.unlocks` and `recalcMods` hooks.
2. Realm rank: county → duchy → kingdom → empire, raised by size and prestige (with coronation events); rank gates titles, laws, council seats and diplomatic weight.
3. Ambitions: rulers pick personal ambitions (build a cathedral, win 3 battles, marry into a great house) that pay prestige/legitimacy.
4. Achievements/score screen: long-term goals and a summary at game end (1453).
Hook into `monthly()` with one line, default the state in `newGame`, back-fill it in `load()`. Give the AI a function to choose research (open an issue for `ws:ai` with the method to call, or add a small clearly separated function).

**Batch 5 (2026-10-27): economy rebalance and crusader states.**
- Diagnosis (all-AI, 20-50 years, `econ.html`-style harness): development grew +5%/year (flat per-province probability, no cap), building levels ×10 in 20 years (AI fills the map, no real upkeep: `upkeep` fields in buildings.js were never charged), market/trade compounding (markets ×37), mines paying 0.3 gold/level, stacked tax/trade modifiers (focus + innovations) additive without limit, cheap `develop`. Result: average net income ×5 in 10 years, ×18 in 20.
- Fixes (economy.js, progression.js, trade_sim.js): development growth base 0.0011 with a soft cap at dev 20, develop cost 60+12·dev²; market tax bonus 8%/level, trade per market 0.2 / port 0.3, gold mines 0.12; building upkeep charged (per-type `upkeep`, default 0.07/level) plus admin 0.02/province and 0.016/dev; `softMult()` on tax and trade modifiers (+100% counts as +56%); commerce saturation (trade + trade-route income divided by 1+T/K, K = 3.2·√provinces, ×1.8 for republics); innovation income bonuses halved, research costs ×2.2; AI trade builds rarer.
- Before (50-year all-AI): development +65% in 20y, income per nation ×18 in 20y. After: development +44% over 50 years, average income ×2.6 (30→75), average net ×1.9, treasuries flat (avg 57, max 318), ~32 innovations per realm.
- Crusader states: after a crusade victory over a Sunni target, if the members hold ≥3 of its former provinces and Jerusalem or Antioch has fallen, that state is revived from those provinces (court, armies, opinion); otherwise the crusaders keep the spoils.
